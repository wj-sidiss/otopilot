# Motion design: devices that tell

A device earns its place when it shows what the camera cannot: a distance, the scale of a number, the inside of a thing, a path through time, a relation between people or places. Pick it from the story's need, never from the list; draw it in the film's own look (its palette, type, texture and light), and land it on its beat (`motion.md`, `sound.md`).

**It elevates when** it tells something new, once, at the moment the viewer needs it, and the picture reads better for it. **It is slop when** it decorates (floating shapes, particles, glow, a generic HUD, lens flares), repeats the same device until it is wallpaper, says what the voice already said, or looks like a template anyone could have dropped in (`looks.md` §8, the cliché table).

## By the job they do

| Job | Devices | Elevates | Slop |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Where, how far** | a route drawn on a map in the film's look · a globe or a map that zooms out to show the distance · a scale bar that grows · a cross-section of height or depth | the distance is the story | a spinning globe as an intro |
| **How much, how many** | a counter that runs · a grid of dots, one per person or thing · two quantities side by side · a bar or a stack that fills | the number is the turn; one comparison the viewer feels | a dashboard of numbers nobody reads |
| **How it works, what is inside** | an exploded view · a cutaway or cross-section · an x-ray through the surface · a diagram that builds line by line · labels on leader lines | the mechanism is the plot | a blueprint texture over everything |
| **When, in what order** | a timeline that scrubs · a clock or countdown plate · a calendar that turns · a before and after wipe | the deadline drives the film | dates flying past as decoration |
| **Who, with whom** | a circle and a name on an archive photo · lines that connect portraits · a family tree or an org chart that builds | a crowd photo where one face matters | every face labelled |
| **Archive pictures** | 2.5D parallax (the photo cut into layers with depth) · then and now overlays · a focus pull through a blurred mask · a traced line that animates over a photo | the still becomes a place the viewer stands in | every photo wobbling in parallax |
| **Documents and words** | a key line highlighted in the real document · telegram or typewriter text · redaction bars · kinetic type for the one line that matters (`typography.md`) | the document is the evidence | a typewriter effect on every caption |
| **Between shots** | a graphic match (a shape in one shot becomes the shape in the next) · a mask reveal through an object · a split screen that compares | the cut makes an argument | a transition on every cut |

## On the card and the board

- **The card names the devices and the job each does** (2–5 in a minute of a film that informs; none, with the reason, in a mood piece), with the beat each lands on. A device without a job is cut.
- **A film that informs** (a documentary, an explainer, a story of facts, or a film without a narrator) gets one direction on the style board led by motion design: its devices named and its references from title sequences and explainers (`asset_search.py search --type typography`), beside the cinema directions.
- **Without a narrator** the devices and the words on screen carry what a narrator would say (`writing.md` §15).
- **Review:** on the contact sheet, each device must answer "what does this tell that the picture alone could not?" in one line; one that cannot is cut, not polished.
