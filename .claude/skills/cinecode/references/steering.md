# Steering: what costs the most in code

Read before the first questions. The user's idea decides; this file only names what code draws badly or what makes a
film drag, so a ★ never walks into it silently.

## 1. What costs the most

- **Realistic people, animals or bodies as the subject** (a walking figure, a breed, an athlete's swing, a historical
  figure's face): code draws them badly, however long it takes. A story that needs people gets a **stylised cast**
  (`characters.md`): it reads, acts and keeps its proportions; or it shows them through what they do and touch.
- **Stillness:** scenes where nothing acts; an even beat (every scene the same length); a long stretch with no new
  event.
- **Length beyond the idea:** an idea stretched past its length repeats its pictures.

## 2. How to steer at the first questions

1. **Read the idea against §1 before offering interpretations.** When it stays clear of §1, offer it straight.
2. **When it leans on §1, the ★ interpretation keeps the idea and its subject and changes only how its people or
   animals are shown,** with one line why; never the subject. The user's original stays as an option with its honest
   cost ("insan figürleri kodla kaba görünür; istersen yine deneriz"). If they choose it, help fully.
3. **Length ★ follows the story:** as long as it needs and no longer; the user's own length always decides.
4. **Name what moves in the logline:** what changes, or, for a mood piece or a portrait, what the viewer is left with.
   Find it before the beats.
