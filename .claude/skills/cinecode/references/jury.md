# The jury: a pinpoint check (optional)

Offered with a delivered draft, never run without the user choosing it. Not a long review: the main chat answers six
questions in two or three minutes from what `make.py` already made, so the next round aims at the few changes that
matter most. No helper, no new render, no new measurement. The idea of an independent, harsh look follows the review
loop of Vincentwei1021/mg-styles-15 (MIT), cut down for speed.

## Look, small

The sheet `make.py` drew (`review/qc-draft-vN/sheet-small.jpg`) and at most three frames at the moments the questions
need (`python3 tools/qc.py thumb <video> 768 <t>`). Read `logs/last-make.txt` for what was measured.

## Six questions, each answered with a timecode and one sentence

1. **Hook:** do the first 2 seconds show the subject acting? If not, what would?
2. **Hero moment:** where is it, and does it land harder than everything around it?
3. **Generic:** is there a frame that could belong to any film (template, stock look, AI default)? Which one?
4. **Weakest five seconds:** where, and why (still, repeated, unclear, too long)?
5. **Sound on the picture:** do the three biggest cuts or impacts have their hit (the sound flags in the log)?
6. **Words:** does any line or card read as generated (`words_check.py` line in the log)?

## Then three fixes at most

Biggest first; each one a timecode, what changes and how, in numbers. At least one raises the wow, not only polish.
One score for the whole draft, honestly anchored (5 student, 6 template, 7 solid professional, 8 high-end agency, 9
award shortlist); first drafts usually land at 5–7.

Show the six answers, the score and the fixes in the user's language, and one question: which fixes to apply (★ all
three). The chosen ones are that round's notes (`notes/round-N.md`).
