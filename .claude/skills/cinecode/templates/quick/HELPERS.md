# film.html helpers at a glance

Read this instead of the starter's code. The FILM section at the top of `film.html` is what a film edits (title, looks, palette, type, cues, scenes, sound tables); everything below it is the engine, used through these helpers. Open a helper's own lines (the Grep tool for `function <name>` in film.html, then those lines with Read) only when its behaviour matters. Scenes are `{ from, to, draw(g, k, t, S), action, in, amb, blur }`; `k` is the scene's progress 0–1, `t` film time, `S` the frame's scale (`S.u` = one unit, `S.W`, `S.H`).

Two traps that cost minutes: JavaScript refuses `-x ** 2` (a syntax error that stops the whole page: write `-(x ** 2)` or `x * x`), and in a shader `pow(x, y)` with a negative `x` gives NaN, a black object (`pow(max(x, 0.0), y)`).

- `const LOOKS`: the eight families (looks.md §1), applied to the whole frame by the look pass: grade (a CSS filter), bloom, texture ('paper' fibre, 'scanlines', 'halftone' dots), vignette, grain (+ grainHold: frames per grain pattern; Infinity = static fibre), hold (drawings per second: 12 = on twos; sound, captions and camera keep 24), line (outline width, share of the short side; 0 = none), steps (toon light bands for shade(); 0 = smooth), palette (the starting PALETTE). `?look=<id>` swaps the family.
- `const FINISH`: finishing, all off unless the user turned it on: shutter (180° motion blur for scenes marked blur: true, `{ deg: 180, samples: 8 }`, 6 for ads; render time × samples there), weave (gate weave in px at 1080, e.g. 0.6), tone (print curve `{ lift: 0.035, knee: 0.72, contrast: 0.12, split: 0.02 }`).
- `const beat = (n) => BEAT0 + n * 60 / BPM, bar = (n) => beat(4 * n)`: the time of beat n and bar n on the music grid; cues and cuts land on them
- `const PALETTE`: the family's palette; replace with the film's own (measured from the board)
- `const TYPE`: The film's type, by role (display: titles and big words · text: subtitles, labels, lower thirds, legal · mono: data, code, timestamps).
- `const CUES`: Cues are shared by picture and sound, so a hit on screen and its sound land on the same frame.
- `const VO`: Narration: one row per line, dur measured from its WAV (ffprobe audio/vo/VO1.wav). The score ducks under each line in renderAudio(); the WAVs are laid on top in the mix step. Empty = no narrator.
- `const SCENES`: Scenes: [from, to) in seconds. Long films: chapters of 15-30 s are an internal structure (one function per scene, for targeted edits); the viewer sees one film: one arc, one look, motifs that travel, connecting transitions.
- `const CAST`: the film's stylised actors, one design each: `{ <name>: { skin, hair, hairStyle: short | bob | long | bun | curly | cap | bald, top, bottom, shoes, outline, build } }` (KIT/references/characters.md)
- `function character(g, S, who, x, y, h, pose = {}, t)`: draws actor `who` with its feet at (x, y), h px tall; pose `{ view: front | three | side | back, face: 1 | -1, emotion (16, see EMOTIONS), action: idle | walk | run | wave | cheer | jump | point | nod | shake | dance | sit, p (seconds into the cycle, 0..1 for jump), talk 0..1, look [x, y], boil }`; blinks, hair sway and boil come from t
- `film.html?modelsheet=<name>`: frame 0 becomes the actor's model sheet (every view, the 16 emotions, the actions), checked before any scene uses the actor
- `const FORMULAS`: math as TeX, only when the film shows formulas: `{ pyth: 'a^2 + b^2 = c^2' }`; typeset once by MathJax (pinned, Apache-2.0, loaded before READY only when there are entries; add its LICENSES.csv row)
- `function formula(g, S, id, x, y, size, color, k = 1, o = {})`: draws FORMULAS[id] centred on (x, y) (`o.align: 'left'`), size = its height in px, tinted, revealed left to right by k; returns its box {x, y, w, h} for labels and arrows
- `const SFX`: Picture-locked sound effects, one row per picture event, on the same CUES: { at, voice, gain, dur, kind, wet }. voice: hit · whoosh (a low swell, peak at at + dur / 2 on the cut) · riser · tick · air · foley (kind: step | tap | cloth); designed sounds are accents, counted by make.py (sound.md §5); wet = send to the shared ROOM (0-1).
- `const ROOM`: One shared reverb space for the whole film (a generated, seeded impulse), so every sound sits in the same room.
- `const ARRANGEMENT`: music: who plays where. Sections `{ section, from, to, parts: [...] }` tile the film from 0 to DURATION; sustained parts run once across neighbouring sections (spans()), beat parts use half-open beat lists (beats()). Checked before any render, skipped when OWN_MUSIC is set.
- `function score(ctx, out)`: Music, in the same timeline: one continuous track following ARRANGEMENT; chapters follow its sections, it never restarts per chapter. ctx is an OfflineAudioContext; out is the music bus.
- `function rng(salt)`: seeded PRNG (mulberry32); call rng(salt) inside draw for a fresh, fixed stream
- `function hash1(n)`: a seeded random number 0–1 for an integer (same seed, same value)
- `function noise1(x)`: smooth seeded noise 0–1 along x: drift, flicker, breathing
- `const clamp = (x, a = 0, b = 1) => Math.min(b, Math.max(a, x))`: keep x between a and b
- `const lerp = (a, b, k) => a + (b - a) * k`: blend from a to b by k
- `const EASE`: `linear`, `in`, `out`, `inOut`, `inertia` (keeps speed at both ends); each `(k) => 0..1`.
- `const MOTION`: things in the frame (motion.md): `at(t, start, dur)` 0..1 progress (a follower uses `at(t - lag, …)`) · `back(k, s = 1.7)` overshoot (s 1 ≈ 4%, 1.7 ≈ 10%, 2.2 ≈ 15%) · `anticipate(k, amount = 0.15, share = 0.25)` wind back, then release · `spring(tau, zeta = 0.7, f = 2)` damped spring tau s after release (zeta 1 firm, 0.7 ≈ 5%, 0.5 ≈ 16%, 0.4 ≈ 25%) · `stagger(i, n, spread, order = 'seq')` start delay (seq, reverse, center, random) · `arc(k, ax, ay, bx, by, bend = 0.15)` a point on a bowed path.
- `VIEW.of(cam, S)`: perspective, a camera standing in a 3D world (y up) at `{ x, y, z }` looking at `{ tx, ty, tz }` with `fov` degrees (keys through `CAM.path` for a moving view); `view.p(x, y, z)` → [sx, sy, scale, depth] or null; `view.sprite(g, x, y, z, size, fn)` places a hand-drawn 2D drawing at its true size and depth; `view.poly(g, pts, fill, stroke)`, `view.line(g, pts, color, w)` draw in perspective; `view.sorted(g, [{ at, draw }])` far to near; `view.haze(depth, near, far)` 0..1 for mixing far things into the air. Use it whenever the camera looks along or around something
- `CAM.path(keys, t)`: one continuous shot: a fresh camera on the path through keys `[{ t, x, y, dist, focal, pan, tilt, roll }]` (missing values keep the previous key), flowing through every key, easing only at the ends; `CAM.follow(c, pos, t, lag = 0.35, k = 1, offset)`: the camera chases a moving subject (pos(t) returns [x, y]) a fraction of a second behind. One scene from 0 to the end, the world in depth planes (`camera-moves.md`, One continuous shot)
- `const CAM`: camera (2.5D): (0,0) is the centre, 1 = the short side, y down; planes at depth z (0 = subject, negative = in front). `base()` a fresh camera, then moves `(c, e, a, b)` with e from EASE: `pan`, `tilt` (+ = up), `dolly` (in: b < a), `zoom` (in: b > a, no parallax), `truck` (+ = right), `crane` (+ = up), `orbit(c, e, a, b, targetZ = 0)` degrees, `dollyZoom(c, e, a, b, subjectZ = 0)` vertigo, `pushTo(c, e, x, y, a, b)` push in on a detail, `fpv(c, e, a, b, bank = 0.15)` fly through planes, `handheld(c, t, amount = 1, salt = 3)` seeded and low-passed, `matchFrom(c, e, dx, dy)` match cut settle, `rackFocus(c, e, a, b, aperture = 1)`.
- `function retime(t, keys)`: Speed ramp / slow motion / time-lapse / freeze frame: remap film time. keys = [[t, speed], ...], speed linear between keys (1 = real time, 0.25 = slow motion, 8 = time-lapse, 0 = freeze). Use the result as the scene's t.
- `function plane(g, S, c, z, fn)`: draw one depth plane through the camera c at depth z (0 = the subject plane); fn(g) draws in world units; out-of-focus planes are blurred (rack focus), so planes at different z give real parallax
- `const TRANSITION`: between rendered scenes A (out) and B (in), k = 0..1 across the window: `dissolve(g, A, B, k)` · `wipe(g, A, B, k, { dir })` · `whip(g, A, B, k, { dir })` whip pan with streaks · `iris(g, A, B, k, { at: [x, y], shape })` B opens in a shape (shape(g, cx, cy, r) draws the path; a circle by default). cut / smash / match need no window: a hard cut on the scene's start (match: B's shape starts where A's was, CAM.matchFrom).
- `const poly`: closed shapes as point lists for morphs: `circle(cx, cy, r, n = 96)`, `rect(x, y, w, h)`, `star(cx, cy, r, points = 5, inner = 0.45)`; any hand-made point list works too. Morph with morphShape(), draw with fillShape().
- `function morphShape(A, B, k, n = 96)`: the outline between shape A and shape B at k (point lists, e.g. from poly); draw it with fillShape
- `function fillShape(g, pts, color, alpha = 1)`: fill a closed point list
- `function lightSweep(g, x, y, w, h, k, o = {})`: Light sweep: a soft band of light crosses a surface (a product, a card, a title) once, k 0 -> 1. clip(g) draws the surface's path; angle in degrees from vertical; width as a share of the surface; strength 0-1.
- `function dot(g, S, x, y, r, color, alpha = 1)`: a filled circle in world units
- `function circle(g, S, x, y, r, color, width)`: a stroked circle in world units
- `function text(g, S, str, x, y, size, color, alpha = 1, tracking = null, o = {})`: world-space type on a plane (titles), x, y = the centre of the cap height. tracking: share of the em, or null for tracking by size. o: role (display by default), weight (any value on a variable wght axis, e.g. breathe(t, BPM, 500, 800)), axes ({ SOFT: 60 } → nearest stop), caps (Turkish-aware), reveal (0..1: units rise in through masks), by ('char' | 'word'), spread, order.
- `function label(g, S, str, x, y, size, color, bg = null, alpha = 1, align = 'left', role = 'text')`: On-screen name (a place, landmark, product, person): a lower third in screen space, unmoved by the camera, in the film's text role.
- `function lazy(make)`: Seeded data made once and reused by every frame (a star field, a layout): const stars = lazy(() => { const r = rng(3); return Array.from({ length: 200 }, () => [r(), r()]); }); then call stars() inside a draw function.
- `function flash(g, S, a)`: a full-frame white flash at opacity a (an impact; land it on a cue)
- `function grain(g, S, frame, amount = 0.05)`: seeded per frame, so re-renders are identical
- `function shade(hex, light)`: the colour under a light value (0 shadow, 0.5 the colour itself, 1 lit), banded into LOOK.steps for toon looks.
- `function inkLine(g, S, color = PALETTE.ink)`: outline the current path in the look's line weight (the look's ink)
- `function lookTexture(g, S)`: the look's texture pass (paper, scanlines, halftone); applied by the engine
- `function vignette(g, S, k)`: darken the frame edges by k
- `const loadType`: Loads every role; any face that fails stops the render (a silent fallback font would change the film's type).
- `function typeFont(T, role, px, o = {})`: font() returns a ctx.font string for a role at px, weight (any number inside the wght axis) and axes (nearest stop; wdth without stops snaps to the CSS stretch keywords). Set it, then draw.
- `function setType(g, T, role, px, o = {})`: set the canvas font to a TYPE role at px size (weight, axes, tracking, caps in o)
- `const breathe = (t, bpm, lo, hi, phase = 0)`: a wght value that breathes with the beat between lo and hi, peaking on each beat (for a variable display face; text().o.weight)
- `function wrap(g, str, maxW, balance = false)`: Lines that fit maxW, with no one-word last line (widow) when it can be avoided; balance: lines of similar length.
- `function headroom(g)`: Ink above the cap height that Turkish and other Latin diacritics need (İ dot, Ğ breve, Ş/Ç cedilla below): masks and boxes use these, never the cap height alone.
- `function typeReveal(g, str, x, y, p, o = {})`: Text in by unit (char | word): each unit rises from a baseline shift inside a mask, staggered across `spread` of the progress p (0..1). ease: an easing from EASE/MOTION; order: seq | reverse | center. Draw after setType().
- `function kineticCaptions(g, T, cap, t, W, H, o = {})`: talking-head captions (optional, typography.md §8) from tools/captions.py: `{ words: [{ w, t0, t1, group, accent, punch }], groups: [{ t0, t1, words: [i…] }] }`.
- `function captionPunch(cap, t, amount = 0.07)`: Punch-in on emphasis: a frame scale for the words marked punch (1 -> 1 + amount in 0.08 s, back over 0.5 s).
- `function pad(ctx, out, t, dur, freqs, gain)`: sound: a sustained chord of freqs from t for dur
- `function kick(ctx, out, t, gain)`: sound: a kick drum at t
- `function tick(ctx, out, t, gain)`: sound: a short tick at t (clock, rhythm)
- `function hit(ctx, out, t, gain)`: sound: an impact at t
- `function riser(ctx, out, t, dur, gain)`: sound: a rising swell over dur, into a cue
- `hz(note)`: Hz of a note name or MIDI number: `hz('A4')` = 440
- `function chord(name, octave = 3, o = {})`: a chord's notes in Hz by name: `chord('Am7')`, `chord('C/E')`, `{ open: true }` spreads it; qualities: major, m, 7, maj7, m7, m9, 9, add9, sus2, sus4, dim, aug, 6, m6, 5
- `function chordAt(prog, t, barsEach = 1, from = 0)`: the chord of a progression (e.g. `['Dmaj7', 'G6', 'Bm7', 'A']`) sounding at t
- `function grid(part, pattern, o = {})`: a groove as steps over one bar inside the part's spans: `'X...x...X...x...'` (X accent, x hit), 16 steps usually, `{ swing: 0.15 }` delays the off sixteenths; jazz and shuffle swing in 12 steps (triplets: ride `x..x.xx..x.x`); returns `[{ t, accent, step }]`
- `function snare(ctx, out, t, gain, o = {})`, `hat(ctx, out, t, gain, { open })`, `clap(ctx, out, t, gain)`: synthesised drums (with `kick`)
- `function sub808(ctx, out, t, dur, f, gain, o = {})`: an 808 style sub bass (`glideFrom`, `glide`, `drive`); `bass(ctx, out, t, dur, f, gain, { cutoff, q })`: a played saw bass; `stab(ctx, out, t, dur, freqs, gain, { cutoff })`: a chord stab
- `function whoosh(ctx, out, t, dur, gain)`: sound: a low swell into a cut, peaking at t + dur / 2 (body under 150 Hz, its harmonics for phone speakers, air 26 dB down); for the few cuts that carry meaning
- `function air(ctx, out, t, dur, gain)`: sound: a real rush of air past the camera (wind, a fast object close by), never the sound of a cut
- `const INSTRUMENTS` and `function sample(ctx, out, name, t, o = {})`: recorded instruments (CC0, `tools/samples.py list` / `get <names>`, fetched only when a film names them): 87 by family: strings (violins, violins-spic, violins-pizz, cellos, cellos-pizz, bass-pizz…), brass (horn, trumpet, trumpet-muted, trombone, tuba-stac…), woodwinds (flute, clarinet, oboe, bassoon-stac, sax, recorder, harmonica…), keys (upright, piano, harpsichord, organ, fm-piano, clavi), plucked (strumstick, folk-harp, zither, psaltery, mbira), mallets (vibes, xylophone, balafon, marimba, glock, tubular-bells), percussion (darbuka, frame-drum, conga, bongos, cajon, snare-drum, hihat, hihat-open, hihat-foot, ride, ride-bell, claps, tambourine, shaker, cowbell, timpani-roll, gong…). Sustained takes last 4 s: a longer hold plays again or sits on a pad. Pitched ones play `o.note` ('E5' or MIDI) from the nearest take, re-pitched (over 4 semitones away stops the render unless `o.stretch`); fast lines on the `-stac`/`-pizz` takes; unpitched ones pick a take by `o.vel` (0..1); `o.gain`, `o.pan`. Name them in INSTRUMENTS (FILM section); renderAudio() loads them before score().
- `function pluck(ctx, out, t, f, gain)`: sound: a plucked note of frequency f (an easy lead)
- `function lead(ctx, out, t, f, dur, gain, cutoff = 2400)`: A sustained lead that stays easy on the ear: triangle + a little saw, low-passed (<= 2.4 kHz), soft 40 ms attack.
- `function foley(ctx, out, t, kind, gain)`: Foley: short filtered noise bursts for contacts. kind: step (soft, low), tap (hard, small), cloth (long, airy).
- `function ambience(ctx, out, from, to, kind, gain, salt)`: Ambience bed for one scene: looped seeded noise shaped per kind, slow seeded drift, 0.3 s crossfades at the edges.
- `function wet(ctx, out, amount)`: Send a voice to the room: wet(ctx, out, 0.3) returns a node that feeds the dry bus and the shared reverb.
- `function spans(part)`: Arrangement helpers. spans(part): [from, to) runs where the part plays, neighbouring sections merged (one voice per run). beats(part, every): grid times inside those runs, half-open, so a boundary beat is played once.
- `function beats(part, every = 60 / BPM)`: the beat times where an ARRANGEMENT part plays (half-open lists, so nothing doubles)
- `function loadAudio(ctx, url)`: a local WAV next to film.html (XHR works on file:// in the capture)
