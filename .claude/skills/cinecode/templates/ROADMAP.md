- **Type:** at step 4b · **Texture:** <grain, tone, anything in the air, as this story wants it, or none, and why> · **Finishing:** at the style frame, the user's choice (motion blur, print tone, gate weave; off by default)
- Every line names things a stranger could draw from it: "cinematic", "moody" or "atmospheric" alone decide nothing.
- Every line carries its reason from this story; shots use what this card names. Variety is the rule: camera moves, shot sizes, light and type change as often as the tempo asks (energetic: a new move or size every 2–4 s; standard: about every 5–6 s; contemplative: long holds that keep revealing), adjacent shots never repeat a move or size, and never the same designed transition twice in a row (a plain cut may follow a cut), and something new reaches the eye or the ear every few seconds. What stays constant is the film's identity: one visual language and a signature (a move, motif, effect or sound) that returns, changed. A device is never added for its own sake; monotony is a fault just like clutter.
